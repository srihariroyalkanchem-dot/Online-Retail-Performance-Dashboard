# Online-Retail-Performance-Dashboard
Developed an interactive E-Commerce Dashboard in Excel to analyze and visualize business performance across sales, customers, products, and order channels. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends over time, best-selling products, order status, customer demographics, and sales distribution across different order modes.

Average Order Value (AOV)

Sales by Gender
Average Customer Rating
# Final Conclusion
The E-Commerce Sales and Customer Insights Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance by analyzing sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and order channels. The analysis reveals top-selling products, key sales channels, customer preferences, and order status patterns. Through interactive filters and visualizations, the dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs effectively, identify growth opportunities, optimize sales strategies, and make informed business decisions to improve overall profitability and customer satisfaction.
